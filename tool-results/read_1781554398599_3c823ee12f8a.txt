     4→[Heading 2] Executive Summary
     5→[None] This roadmap covers 9 backend-only sprints from June 16 to August 15, 2025, targeting production readiness for the Chatco jeepney tracking system. The frontend (Next.js) is already implemented; this plan exclusively addresses the Laravel backend that the frontend expects to connect to.
     6→[None] Key architectural decisions confirmed throughout all sprints:
     7→[List Paragraph] Wallet system is fully and permanently removed — no internal balance, no top-up, no ledger
     8→[List Paragraph] GCash QR is direct external payment via PayMongo only — no internal money movement
     9→[List Paragraph] All jeepney units are visible to commuters at all times — no distance filter on visibility
    10→[List Paragraph] 1KM hail restriction is enforced server-side only — frontend is display-only
    11→[List Paragraph] Backend is the single source of truth — frontend never bypasses backend validation
